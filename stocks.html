🧠 How the signal works

For each ticker the app pulls recent headlines and scores them against a lexicon of bullish words (beats, surges, upgrade, record, growth…) and bearish words (misses, plunges, downgrade, lawsuit, layoffs…). That news sentiment is blended with the day's price change to produce a score from −1 to +1: > +0.25 → BUY, < −0.25 → SELL, otherwise HOLD. The confidence bar reflects how strong and consistent that signal is — never a guarantee.
